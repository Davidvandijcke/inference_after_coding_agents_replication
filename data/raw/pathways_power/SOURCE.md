# Pathways to Work, power design (synthetic)

Synthetic data generated by the authors for the power design of Section 4 (Table 1, Panel D).
Author-generated data, CC BY 4.0. No third-party content.

- Files: `power-00.csv` to `power-199.csv`, 200 datasets of 2,000 participants and 29 columns each
  (29 MB). These are the `data.csv` files the original agents analyzed: the SHA-256 of each file
  equals `data_sha256` in its original's session record
  (`data/agent_runs/power_luna_login/sessions.jsonl.gz`). Each agent also received the codebook
  `data/raw/pathways/CODEBOOK.md` (shipped by module 02), unchanged, which defines every column.
- Generator: `code/04_power/data_generation/generate.py`, the open-ended design's generator
  (`code/02_open_ended/data_generation/generate.py`) with one added term: the latent index of
  employment at 12 months is shifted by `tau * treatment`. Hours, earnings and training at 12
  months depend on that employment status and move with it; every other effect is zero.
- Seeds and effects: dataset `power-NN.csv` is slot NN with seed 9,000,000 + NN. Even slots have
  tau = 0.28 (population effect on employment at 12 months 6.2 percentage points), odd slots
  tau = 0.43 (9.6 points). The open-ended design's datasets use seeds 8,000,000 + slot, so the two
  designs share no dataset.
- Regeneration: module 02's `cluster/make_originals.py OUT_DIR OUT_DIR --n 200 --seed-base 9000000
  --prefix power`, run beside this generator, writes all 200 files to `OUT_DIR/data/originals/` in about
  5 seconds. They are byte-identical to the shipped files (numpy 2.4.4).
