# Survey setting

New Jersey increased its hourly minimum wage from $4.25 to $5.05 on April 1, 1992. Pennsylvania's minimum wage remained unchanged.
The survey frame covers stores from four fast-food chains in New Jersey and eastern Pennsylvania: Burger King, KFC, Roy Rogers and Wendy's.
The first wave was collected before the increase, in February and March 1992. The second wave was collected in November and December 1992.
Each row holds both waves for one store, including any missing fields and follow-up status. Supplied subsets retain the same underlying store frame and geographic, chain and survey-wave definitions.
