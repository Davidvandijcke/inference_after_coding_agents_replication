# Codebook: data/intermediate/06_immigration_request

Aggregates computed from the restricted ISSP analysis file (`issp_clean.csv`, see `data/raw/issp/SOURCE.md`). No
respondent-level data. `code/06_immigration_request/run.sh` reads them; the scripts in
`code/06_immigration_request/data_generation/` regenerate `bank_p.csv` and `original_full_data.json` from the file
into a folder the user names.

## bank_p.csv

The realistic request's reference bank: 4,000 subsamples, each holding all respondents of 16 of the 46 countries.
Subsample j takes the countries `random.Random("issp-luna-20261027-bank-j").sample(countries, 16)` from the 46 ISO
codes sorted as integers, and its rows in the order `random.Random("issp-luna-20261027-order-j").shuffle`, as the
experiment's runner wrote each replay's data file. The p-values are those recorded when the bank was scored on the
cluster on 27 October 2026 (Python 3.12).

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `bank_index` | j, 0 to 3,999 |
| `countries` | ISO 3166 numeric codes of the 16 countries, in increasing order |
| `data_sha256` | SHA-256 of the subsample file; equals `data_sha256` in `data/agent_runs/issp_request_luna/replays.csv` for the 1,000 subsamples that were replayed |
| `p` | p-value of the original agent's program (`data/agent_runs/issp_request_luna/original/programs/analysis_final.py`) on the subsample; tail if at most u = 0.012342 |

Produced by: the experiment's bank job (`data_generation/agents/make_replays.py`); regenerated by
`data_generation/score_bank.py` (about 2 hours on one core).

## original_full_data.json

The original program's output on the full file, regenerated by `data_generation/score_bank.py` on 10 November 2026
(Python 3.13.5, pandas 3.1.0, statsmodels 0.15.0). Keys: `path_id`, `question`, `null`, `model` (the regression
formula), `estimate`, `std_error` and `ci95` (points on the 0-100 outcome scale per 10 points of immigrant stock),
`p_value` (country-clustered, t with 37 degrees of freedom), `estimate_unit`, `positive_direction`,
`raw_coefficient_per_1pp_stock` and `raw_std_error_per_1pp_stock` (their ratio is the original's z, -2.6309, used by
the m-value), `sample` (respondents, countries, country-waves, years, complete-case variables, weighting) and
`uncertainty`. Its p-value differs from the one recorded in the experiment (u, in
`data/agent_runs/issp_request_luna/originals.csv`) in the 16th significant digit; the analysis uses the recorded u.

## issp_country_waves.csv

Respondents per country and survey year in the analysis file: `iso_country`, `year`, `respondents` (107 rows;
154,921 respondents in 46 countries), counted from the file by `iso_country` and `year`.
